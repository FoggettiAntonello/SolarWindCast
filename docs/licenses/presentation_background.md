A soft understated golden sun in the upper-right corner and three elegant realistic wind turbines along the far-right lower edge and distant rolling land near the bottom. Turbines must have physically coherent three-blade rotors. Very low contrast, airy, refined, no dark silhouettes. Leave the central 85 percent and left side almost blank ivory for black text and data charts; decorative elements concentrated near the outer right margin and bottom. Subtle paper texture, clean corporate presentation style, not cartoonish, no text, no logos, no numbers, no graphs, no fake data. Landscape wide 16:9 composition.
